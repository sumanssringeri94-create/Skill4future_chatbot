=== PROGRAM OVERVIEW ===
Skills4Future is a CSR initiative by Shell India Markets Private Limited, implemented by
Edunet Foundation. It equips students and faculty with Green Skills and Artificial
Intelligence (AI) capabilities, focused on employability and real-world, project-based
learning. The program is offered across 4 states in India (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka,
Maharashtra, Gujarat), with the AICTE Internship offered PAN India.

The program has three tracks:
1. Student Development Program (SDP) — Foundation course and Advanced course
2. Shell-Edunet Skills4Future AICTE Internship
3. Faculty Development Program (FDP)

All tracks are completely FREE for eligible participants (this is a CSR initiative).

=== STUDENT DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (SDP) ===

-- Foundation Course --
Duration: 50 hours total (45 hours technical skills + 5 hours case study)
Eligibility:
- 17 years or older
- Enrolled in year 2, 3, or 4 of an engineering degree in: Computer Engineering, IT,
  Electronics & Communication, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Data Science
- Willing to commit the required hours alongside regular academics
- Basic computer operating/programming skills
- Access to a computer/laptop with internet (personal or via institution)

Foundation Course modules:
1. Introduction to Green Skilling and AI — 6 hrs
2. Learning Python and Data Visualisation for environmental applications — 14 hrs
3. Applying Machine Learning in environmental monitoring — 10 hrs
4. Deep Learning and Gen AI for sustainable solutions — 10 hrs
5. AI Ethics, Fairness, and Sustainability — 5 hrs
6. Case Study — 5 hrs
Total: 50 hrs

-- Advanced Course --
Duration: 160 hours total (100 hrs technical skills + 20 hrs professional/employability
skills + 40 hrs capstone project)
Eligibility:
- 18 years or older
- Enrolled in pre-final or final year of engineering in: Computer Engineering, IT,
  Electronics & Communication, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Data Science
- Willing to pursue a career in Green Skills and AI
- Commit the required hours alongside regular academics
- Basic computer operating/programming skills
- Access to a computer/laptop with internet

Advanced Course modules:
1. Foundation of Green Skilling, Sustainability and AI Contributions — 14 hrs
2. Fundamentals of AI, Machine Learning and Python Programming — 23 hrs
3. Applied Data Analytics with Python for Environmental Applications — 15 hrs
4. Implementation and Deployment of ML and Deep Learning Algorithms — 20 hrs
5. AI for Green Applications — 10 hrs
6. Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) for Sustainable Solutions — 10 hrs
7. AI Ethics, Fairness, and Sustainability — 8 hrs
8. Employability skills — 20 hrs
9. Capstone project — 40 hrs
Total: 160 hrs

-- General SDP Info --
- Training delivery: through Master Trainers at the student's own institution
- Training mode: Hybrid (virtual/in-person)
- Trainers: Edunet trainers with industry experience in Green Skills and AI
- Fee: Completely free for eligible participants
- Enrollment: Advanced course → register at skills4future.in/advanced-student-registration
              Foundation course → register at skills4future.in/foundation-student-registration

-- Certificates & Benefits (both courses) --
- Free co-branded certificate from Shell and Edunet
- Industry-relevant, project-based skill building
- Mentorship from industry experts (Advanced course)
- LMS access for continuous learning
- Advanced course only: placement support after completion

Certificate eligibility criteria:
- Students with 70% or higher attendance qualify for the final assessment
- Score 60% or more in final assessment → Completion Certificate
- Score below 60% but attended → Participation Certificate

Placement support (Advanced course only) requires ALL of:
- At least 70% attendance
- More than 60% score in assessment
- Submission of project work

=== AICTE INTERNSHIP (Shell-Edunet Skills4Future) ===
- Purpose: Bridges the employability gap with AI + Green Skills experience
- Cost: Completely free — no charges to participate
- Stipend: NOT provided by the organization
- Letter of Reference (LOR): NOT provided upon completion
- Job guarantee: NOT guaranteed after completion
- Class frequency: 3 times a week, fixed session timings (cannot be changed)
- Project deadlines: Fixed — late submissions are not accepted
- Duration: Indicative 4-week virtual internship (subject to Edunet's operational discretion)
- Example past project areas: Energy Consumption Trend Analysis with Power BI, Indian
  Agriculture Sector analysis with Power BI, Healthcare Prediction on Diabetic Patients
  using Python, Waste Classification using Image Processing and Python, Air Quality
  Index Prediction with Python
- Value: Builds CV, provides a certificate, and builds real knowledge/skillset even
  without a stipend or guaranteed job

=== FACULTY DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (FDP) ===
- Purpose: Upskills faculty from AICTE-approved engineering institutions in emerging
  tech (Green Skills + AI)
- Eligibility: Faculty from AICTE-approved institutions in Computer Engineering, IT,
  Electronics & Communication, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Data Science
- Region: Only in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Gujarat
- Training mode: In-person, delivered by a Master Trainer (subject matter expert) at
  the institution
- Duration: 20 hours, spread across 3–5 days
- Topic: "Advanced Machine Learning with Python and Green Technologies"
- Fee: Free (CSR initiative of Shell India and Edunet Foundation)
- Enrollment: Register at skills4future.in/fdp-registration/batch_code
- Certificate: Free joint certificate from Shell India and Edunet Foundation, issued
  digitally via the Learning Management System (LMS), for faculty who qualify
- Assessment criteria: 60% or higher attendance required to sit the final assessment.
  Score 60%+ → Completion Certificate. Below 60% → Participation Certificate.
- Benefits: Real-world exposure to Green Skills & AI technologies, helps bridge the
  gap between academic learning and industry needs, empowers faculty to better
  prepare students for the job market
